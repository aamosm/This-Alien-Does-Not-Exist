    How to play

    You control the left creature. Get the opponent's health to zero. Both choose a move before the round plays; the faster creature goes first. Guard protects for the whole round.

    Select a move with a click or its key, then press Enter to use it. Energy pays for moves. Breathe restores energy; Rest heals, but leaves you asleep for two turns. Passive abilities work automatically.

    Try a sequence. Focus → Strike boosts two attacks. Irritant spray → Guard lets irritation keep hurting the opponent while you defend. Sweep → Lunge can help you attack first next round. These are ordinary moves used together, not hidden button combinations.

    Finding a fight. New opponent keeps your creature and finds another. Ordinary creatures share an 800-point stat budget; their strengths differ. Terrain and activity hours give small advantages. You can choose them above or leave them random. Every creature starts awake.

    Seeds. A creature seed identifies one creature. The encounter code controls chance; it is not a creature seed. You never need to type either to play. Share keeps the setup and any moves already played, so someone else can replay them.

    Keys. 1–6 select the main moves. Q W E R T Y U select the extra moves. Enter starts or plays one round. N finds a new opponent. A toggles automatic play. ? opens this help. Esc closes help or pauses automatic play. Shortcuts stay off while typing.

    Rematch repeats the same setup and rolls; changing your moves can change the outcome. Healing halves after round 30, stops after round 45, and a fight still going at round 60 is a draw.
