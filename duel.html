How this encounter works

1. Creature seeds identify fighters. They determine anatomy, ratings, two passive abilities and activity preference. You control the left creature; the computer controls the right.

2. Choose an arena. Terrain and time give small, visible advantages. “From encounter code” selects them reproducibly. Creatures start awake, even outside their preferred active hours.

3. Select a move, then play the round. Both creatures commit before either acts. Speed determines attack order. Guard protects for the whole round. Health reaching zero ends the fight; energy pays for moves.

4. The encounter code controls chance. It is not a creature seed or a power level. Same creatures, code, terrain, time, rules and move sequence give the same outcome. Changing your moves can change the winner. “New code” creates a different encounter.

Sleep and recovery. Breathe restores energy only. Rest heals but leaves you asleep for two turns. While asleep, use the Sleep button to advance a round. Passives trigger automatically; their effects appear in the log.

Autoplay plays visibly. Pause stops before the next round. Replay links retain the moves already played. After round 30 healing is halved; after round 45 it stops. Unfinished fights draw after 60 rounds.

Encounter settings

Controls chance and automatic conditions—not creature strength. Preview applies your settings and resets the fight.
